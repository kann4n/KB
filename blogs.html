Picking Up a Pen After Ten Years

Mar 30, 2026

I used to draw a lot as a kid, then stopped. Coming back as an adult is humbling — my hands remember wanting to draw better than they remember how.

The fix has been small and consistent: one little sketch a day, pixel art or pencil, no pressure for it to be good.
